## 1. Executive Summary

During the recent end-to-end evaluation, the human reviewer evaluated an output video of approximately 43 seconds (`streameditor_e2e_real_edit.mp4`) generated from an authentic ~5.2-hour livestream. The reviewer correctly observed that:
1. The streamer's opening greeting was cut.
2. The beginning of the game was omitted, removing critical context.
3. The video consisted of disconnected highlights rather than a coherent narrative.
4. The system failed to distinguish between necessary narrative context, repetitive gameplay, and genuine entertainment.
5. The final video was vastly shorter than the intended long-form YouTube editorial product (~20–40 minutes).

This audit conducted an exhaustive forensic trace of the pipeline execution, code paths, configuration models, and data artifacts to establish the definitive root cause.

## 3. Why the Streamer Greeting and Game Start Were Cut

### 3.1 The Greeting Truncation ([0.0s – 48.0s])
- **In the master stream (`source.mp4`):**
  - `[0.0s – 25.0s]`: Stream starting screen and countdown.
  - `[25.0s – 48.0s]`: Streamer appears on webcam, warmly greets the stream chat, says hello to viewers, and introduces the session.
- **In the 43s output:**
  - Clip 1 was declared with `source_start = 48.0s` and `source_end = 66.5s`.
  - The script completely skipped `[0.0s, 48.0s]`, cutting in media res when the streamer was talking about audio settings.
- **In the Human Reference (`edited.mp4`):**
  - The human editor included the greeting in the opening 15 seconds (`edit: [2.5s – 14.0s] <- source: [20.0s – 35.0s]`). The human editor considered this essential social context.

### 3.2 The Game Start Truncation ([66.5s – 333.0s])
- **In the master stream:**
  - `[66.5s – 210.0s]`: Streamer explains the game they are about to play (*Weed Shop 3*), explains the objectives, and launches into the game world.
  - `[210.0s – 330.0s]`: Walking into the shop, showing the setup, explaining the mechanics.
- **In the 43s output:**
  - A massive 266.5-second temporal jump occurred between Clip 1 (`end = 66.5s`) and Clip 2 (`start = 333.0s`).
  - The viewer was suddenly thrust into cutting plants with shears with zero narrative setup.
- **In the Human Reference:**
  - The human editor kept `[210.0s – 330.0s]` (`edit: [44.5s – 57.0s]`) to show the streamer entering the dispensary and setting up before the first customer/harvest.

## 5. Architectural Failure Analysis in M4/M5

The core structural failure that prevented long-form editorial intelligence is located in M4 and M5:

1. **Absence of Narrative Anchor Nodes in M4**:
   `StoryGraph` treats all candidates as interchangeable nodes scored by excitement. It lacks structural anchor types:
   - `STREAM_OPENING_ANCHOR`: Streamer introduction and greeting.
   - `GAME_START_ANCHOR`: Transition into the game, setting the primary objective.
   - `SESSION_OUTRO_ANCHOR`: Conclusion and sign-off.
   Without anchor typing, M5 optimization cannot protect these low-excitement, high-necessity nodes.

2. **Flawed Pruning Hierarchy in M5**:
   In `packages/editorial/src/stream_editor/editorial/planning/optimizer.py`:
   ```python
   PRUNE_ORDER = [
       ClipPriority.context_only,  # <-- Pruned first!
       ClipPriority.low,
       ClipPriority.medium,
       ClipPriority.high,
   ]
   ```
   When duration constraints are enforced, any clip marked `context_only` is discarded immediately before touching any medium or high excitement clip. This guarantees that introductions, setups, and explanations are stripped away whenever budget is limited.

3. **Missing Editing Presets in Contracts**:
   In `packages/contracts/src/stream_editor/contracts/edit_plan.py`, `EditPlanConfig` only had a generic `PlanningProfile` (`compact`, `balanced`, `comprehensive`) with a single default target duration (`9000.0s`). It lacked explicit editorial goal contracts:
   - `SHORT_HIGHLIGHT` (60s – 180s): For YouTube Shorts / TikTok.
   - `LONG_FORM_SUMMARY` (600s – 1200s): For a 10–20 minute condensed episode.
   - `FULL_EDITED_SESSION` (1800s – 2700s): For a 30–45 minute comprehensive edit matching human reference style.

## 6. Recommendations & Remediation Roadmap

1. **Contracts Update**: Formally declare `EditingGoal` in `edit_plan.py` supporting `SHORT_HIGHLIGHT`, `LONG_FORM_SUMMARY`, and `FULL_EDITED_SESSION`.
2. **Anchor Node Protection**: Add `is_narrative_anchor` and `ClipPriority.essential` to opening greetings and game start intervals, preventing them from being pruned by the duration optimizer.
3. **Calibrate Optimizer**: Update `DeterministicDurationOptimizer` so that narrative anchors with `ClipPriority.essential` are NEVER pruned regardless of duration pressure.
4. **Independent Benchmark**: Build and execute `E2E-LONGFORM-REFERENCE-001` to compare AI selections against the Human Edit Decision Map across the entire 5.19-hour timeline.
