# Research Pipeline

Extracting EditDNA:
1. Input: Original VOD + edited final video.
2. Transcript Alignment: Map the edited audio back to the raw transcript.
3. Timeline Mapping: Determine what was retained and what was cut.
4. Research Findings: Generate hypotheses on why certain edits were made.
5. Human Review: User reviews findings in the inbox.
6. Approved Rules: Findings are promoted to the Editorial Bible.
