# MVP Scope

The MVP encompasses M0 through M5. It must be able to take a raw VOD, analyze the audio and visuals, propose a basic cut (removing dead air, keeping loud/active moments), render a preview, allow human tweaking, and export an MP4.

*No advanced effects or EditDNA learning in the MVP.*
