# StreamEditor AI Knowledge Vault

Welcome to the StreamEditor AI knowledge vault. This Obsidian vault is the canonical source of truth for the product vision, architecture, editorial rules, and project status.

## What is StreamEditor AI?
StreamEditor AI is a system designed to reconstruct the livestream experience by intelligently editing VODs (Video on Demand). It is **not** a simple highlight generator. It preserves narrative, humor, callbacks, and chat interactions, approximating the editorial judgment of a careful human YouTube editor.
