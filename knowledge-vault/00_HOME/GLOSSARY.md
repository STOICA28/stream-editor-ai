# Glossary

- **VOD:** Video on Demand. The raw livestream recording.
- **EditPlan:** The validated schema representing a set of editing decisions.
- **TimelineEvent:** A discrete event occurring at a specific time in the VOD (e.g., laughter, scene change).
- **CandidateSegment:** A potential clip identified for inclusion in the final edit.
- **StoryGraph:** A graph representing the narrative dependencies between segments.
- **NarrativeNode:** A node in the StoryGraph (setup, payoff, etc.).
- **EditDNA:** The underlying patterns extracted from a human-edited reference video.
- **VaultIndex:** The indexed search database over the Obsidian vault.
- **AnalysisProxy:** A lower-resolution version of the VOD used for AI analysis to save costs.
- **ModelProvider:** The abstraction layer over AI models.
- **StyleProfile:** The specific editing style parameters for a channel or editor.
- **FeedbackEvent:** A record of human modification to an AI proposed EditPlan.
- **EffectAPI:** The enumerated list of supported visual/audio effects.
- **ReferenceProject:** A project used solely to extract EditDNA.
- **ResearchFinding:** A hypothesis or observation generated by the Research Agent.
- **VaultCurator:** The agent responsible for maintaining this knowledge vault.
