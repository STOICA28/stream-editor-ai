# Process Video

1. Open Web UI.
2. Create a new Project.
3. Upload or link the raw VOD.
4. Click "Start Processing".
5. Monitor the progress bar (polls Job Progress API).
6. Once complete, navigate to "Review Edit" to see the proposed EditPlan.
