# Editorial Evaluation

How we score AI performance:
1. **Setup/Payoff Coverage:** Did the AI include the setup for every payoff it kept?
2. **Clip Continuity:** Are there jarring jump cuts without transitions?
3. **Narrative Coherence Score:** Human reviewer score (1-5) on the overall flow.
