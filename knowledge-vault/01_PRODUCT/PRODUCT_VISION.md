# Product Vision

StreamEditor AI is a sophisticated AI editing assistant designed to reconstruct the livestream experience. 

It is **NOT a highlight generator**. Highlighting just extracts loud moments. StreamEditor AI aims to approximate the editorial judgment of a careful human YouTube editor. 

It achieves this by:
- Preserving narrative context.
- Understanding humor structure (setup and payoff).
- Maintaining running jokes.
- Ensuring emotional transitions are smooth.
- Incorporating stream chat as an active participant in the story.
