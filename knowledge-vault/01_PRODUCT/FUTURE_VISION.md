# Future Vision (Post-MVP)

- **Story Graph:** Advanced narrative dependency tracking.
- **EditDNA Learning:** Fully automated extraction of style from reference videos.
- **Critic Agent:** Iterative self-correction of edits before human review.
- **Personal Style Profiles:** Saving and applying different creators' styles.
- **Fine-tuning Feedback:** Using human corrections to improve future AI proposals.
- **Team Collaboration:** Multi-user projects.
