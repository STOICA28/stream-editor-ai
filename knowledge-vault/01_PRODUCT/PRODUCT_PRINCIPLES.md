# Product Principles

- **AI decides, deterministic software executes:** AI outputs structured schemas, never arbitrary commands.
- **Preserve narrative context over highlight extraction:** Context is king.
- **Resumable and idempotent pipelines:** Any job step can be retried safely.
- **Evidence-based editorial rules:** Rules must come from observed EditDNA.
- **Human always in control:** AI proposes, human approves.
- **Obsidian vault is source of truth:** Documentation drives the system.
- **Version everything:** Models, prompts, schemas, EditPlans.
