# Humor Structure

Humor in streams usually follows this structure:
`Setup -> Delivery -> Reaction -> Chat Echo`

- **Rule:** Never cut the reaction. The streamer's reaction or the chat's reaction is the "audience laugh track".
- **Delayed Setups:** Sometimes the setup happens 5 minutes before the punchline. Use `TimelineEvent` links to ensure the setup is retained.
