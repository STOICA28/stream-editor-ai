# Pacing

- **Rhythm variation:** Break up long segments with appropriate pacing adjustments.
- **Speed-up vs Cut:** Consider whether boring but necessary progression should be sped up rather than cut entirely.
- **Configurable Thresholds:** Specific limits for clip duration, uninterrupted silence, and speed-up factors must remain configurable and should be validated through EditDNA research rather than hardcoded.
