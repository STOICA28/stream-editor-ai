# Storytelling

Narrative arc principles apply even to gaming VODs:
1. **Introduction:** Setting the goal.
2. **Rising Action:** The struggles and minor wins.
3. **Climax:** The boss fight or major event.
4. **Resolution:** The aftermath and reflection.

Long-form editing should feel like a complete, coherent story.
