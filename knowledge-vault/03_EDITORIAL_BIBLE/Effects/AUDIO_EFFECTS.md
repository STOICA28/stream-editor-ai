# Audio Effects

- **Audio Gain:** Increase for quiet whispers, decrease for extremely loud screams.
- **Audio Duck:** Automatically lower background music or game audio when the streamer speaks.
- **Fades:** Use 0.5s crossfades between hard cuts if audio is jarring. Never create abrupt pops.
