# Face Zoom

**When to use:**
- Genuine surprise/reaction.
- Punchline delivery.
- Intense emotional moment.

**Constraints:**
- Scale: 1.2x to 2.0x.
- Duration: 0.5s to 5.0s.
- Interpolation: Smooth (ease-in-out).
- Must be centered on the detected face region.
