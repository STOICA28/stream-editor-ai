# Emotional Editing

- **Emotional arc editing:** Avoid jumping directly from high energy (screaming/hype) to dead silence or sad moments without a transition.
- **Preserve emotional continuity.**
- **Identify emotional peaks** and ensure they are given enough "breathing room" (post-context).
