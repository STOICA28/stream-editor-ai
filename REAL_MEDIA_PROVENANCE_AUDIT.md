A formal review of the EXP-002 A/B evaluation package identified critical design and methodology defects:

4. **Tooling Burden:** Reviewers were required to parse complex JSON files (`exp002_real_ab_reviewer_package.json`) to perform editorial evaluations.

This document conducts a complete cryptographic, visual, and pipeline audit of every media file in the repository, formally invalidates all claims derived from synthetic fixtures in real benchmarks, and defines the reset protocol.

## 3. Root Cause Analysis of Methodology Failures

### 3.1. Misclassification of Synthetic Fixture `source_0.mp4` as `real-eval-002`
- **Root Cause:** In earlier development (M10.2), `source_0.mp4` was generated by `SyntheticMediaGenerator` to provide an exact oracle ground truth for reverse-edit alignment. In M13 and subsequent EXP-002 evaluations, `source_0.mp4` was reused in benchmark scripts under the assumed label `"real-eval-002"` because it was believed to represent a conversational audio beat.
- **Visual Reality:** Frames of `source_0.mp4` contain no human being or video stream. They are black 360p frames with synthetic geometric primitives (a red circle and a green box labeled "FACE").
- **Formal Invalidation:**  
  All claims asserting that EXP-002 demonstrated an editorial advantage on `real-eval-002` are **FORMALLY REVOKED AND INVALIDATED**. Synthetic fixtures are strictly restricted to unit and regression testing.

### 3.2. Evaluation of Diagnostic Boundary Trims instead of Editorial Edits
- **Root Cause:** EXP-002 measures whether Stage M3 clusters related candidate windows (e.g. $[12.0, 19.5]$ and $[20.1, 28.0]$ into a single $[12.0, 28.0]$ candidate). When these candidate intervals were rendered into 16-second comparison clips, the only difference between Version A and Version B was a subsecond jump-cut vs. a continuous 0.6s pause.
- **Reviewer Impact:** A human reviewer evaluating a 16-second video cannot evaluate narrative structure, pacing, or storytelling because no narrative arc exists in a single isolated beat.
- **Separation Mandate:** Diagnostic boundary decisions in M3 must be clearly presented with their surrounding unedited context and precise subsecond descriptions, while overall editing quality must be demonstrated through a complete end-to-end pipeline execution on multi-minute authentic footage.

### 3.3. Provenance Linkage between Recordings
- **Audit Finding:** `tests/fixtures/real_clutch_reaction.mp4` is an extract from `source_short.mp4` ($[330.0\text{s}, 365.0\text{s}]$), which itself is the first 600 seconds of `data/source_5hr.mp4`.
- **Methodological Disclosure:** The test suite did not test three independent broadcasts; it tested different temporal windows of the **same single master livestream broadcast**. This limitation is permanently documented.

## 4. Reset & Remediation Actions

1. **Synthetic Isolation:** `tests/fixtures/source_0.mp4`, `tests/fixtures/source.mp4`, `tests/fixtures/edited_0.mp4`, and `tests/fixtures/edited.mp4` are quarantined strictly to unit and integration test suites. They are permanently excluded from all human review packages.
2. **Authentic Source Window:** All editorial comparisons and demonstrations are established exclusively on authentic footage from `source_short.mp4` (10 minutes continuous, 1080p60 AV1).
3. **Context-Rich A/B Packaging:** Every M3 evaluation case must include:
   - A dedicated **Source Context Video** showing the unedited livestream segment ($30\text{s} - 55\text{s}$) with surrounding gameplay and commentary.
   - Version A and Version B blinded outputs.
   - An explicit explanation of the candidate boundary decision being tested.
   - Declaration of exact subsecond timing differences.
4. **End-to-End Product Demonstration:** A separate, complete pipeline execution (M1 $\to$ M9) on the 10-minute authentic footage window producing a finished, multi-clip edited video demonstrating highlight selection, pacing, and visual effects.
5. **Interactive Review Application:** A zero-friction local HTML application (`exp002_ab_review_app.html`) providing synchronized video players, clear context, and single-click evaluation without opening raw JSON files.
